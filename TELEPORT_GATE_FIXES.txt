KnightImperia 1298 Server - Teleport / Warp Gate fixes

Reference client:
1298-Client-Source-master/Client/WarFare/GameProcMain.cpp

Fixes applied:
1. WIZ_WARP_LIST response now includes the 16-bit destination max-user field that the 1298 client reads after the destination zone.
2. WIZ_ZONE_CHANGE / ZONE_CHANGE_TELEPORT now uses the 1298 client zone encoding:
   zoneHigh = zone / 10
   zoneLow  = zone % 10
3. Zone-change X/Z are emitted as unsigned 16-bit coordinates, matching the client parser.
4. Destination Y is recalculated from the destination map before WIZ_ZONE_CHANGE is sent, preventing stale Y from the previous zone during teleport-gate warps.
5. Existing AIServer/Ebenezer/Client NPC packet fixes in the previous fixed archive are preserved.

No client source files were modified.
